What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is one of the latest innovations in the dental implant industry. Less implants are required to keep the denture intact. Four implants are placed at strategic distances in the jaw, requiring less bone than traditional implant approaches. This technique is ideal for patients with 12 or more missing teeth.

The angled posterior implants provide key benefits: they avoid vital anatomical structures, reduce cantilevers, and reduce the need for bone grafting. Computer-aided diagnostics and Nobel Guide systems are used for precise planning. The implants can be immediately loaded for shorter treatment times.

The Treatment Process

Your journey from missing teeth to a full smile in one day

1

Computer-Aided Diagnostics

3D CBCT scans and Nobel Guide computer-aided diagnostics are used to virtually plan every implant position at strategic distances for optimal support.

2

Strategic Implant Placement

Four implants are placed at strategic distances — two straight in the anterior and two angled in the posterior to avoid anatomical structures and reduce the need for bone grafting.

3

Immediate Loading

The implants are immediately loaded with a fixed bridge for shorter treatment times. You leave the clinic with a functional, aesthetic smile on the same day.

4

Final Prosthesis

After 3-6 months of healing and osseointegration, the temporary bridge is replaced with your final, precision-milled prosthesis for a lifetime of confident smiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

All-on-4 is one of the latest innovations in the dental implant industry. Less implants are required to keep the denture intact. The angled posterior implants avoid anatomical structures, reduce cantilevers, and reduce the need for bone grafting.

All-on-4 is ideal for patients with 12 or more missing teeth. The technique requires less bone than traditional approaches, and the angled posterior implants engage denser bone areas, often eliminating the need for bone grafting.

Immediate loading means the implants are loaded with a fixed bridge on the same day as surgery, resulting in shorter treatment times. You leave the clinic with functional teeth immediately after the procedure.

We use computer-aided diagnostics and Nobel Guide systems for precise 3D planning of every implant position. This ensures each implant is placed at the optimal strategic distance for maximum support and longevity.

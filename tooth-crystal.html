Tooth Crystal / Tooth Jewelry

Add a dazzling accent to your smile with premium dental crystals and gems — safe, painless, and completely removable whenever you choose.

About This Treatment

Dental Jewelry & Tooth Enhancements

Tooth jewelry is a non-invasive, pain-free cosmetic procedure that adds a small, stylish gem or crystal to your teeth. From Swarovski crystals to custom gold or diamond accents, our dental jewelry options let you express your personality with a dazzling smile.

Whether you prefer a temporary or permanent design, we offer customizable options that are safe and cause no damage to your natural teeth. Book a consultation to explore custom designs, pricing, and payment plans for your dream smile.

  • No drilling, no damage — safe, pain-free application
  • Customizable options — Swarovski crystals, gold or diamond accents
  • Choose between removable or long-lasting designs
  • Diamond tooth implants — real diamonds within crowns for premium, long-lasting shine
  • Gold tooth crowns — durable, resistant to wear, chipping and fractures
How It Works

The Application Process

1

Crystal Selection

Choose from Swarovski crystals, custom gold accents, or real diamond options. We help you select the perfect design, size, and placement for your smile.

2

Tooth Preparation

The tooth surface is gently cleaned and conditioned. No drilling or enamel removal is involved — the procedure is completely non-invasive and pain-free.

3

Crystal Bonding

Using dental-grade adhesive, the crystal or gem is precisely positioned and bonded. A curing light secures the bond in seconds with no damage to the tooth.

4

Diamond & Gold Options

For premium enhancements, choose diamond tooth implants with real diamonds placed within crowns, or gold tooth crowns that offer minimal tooth reduction and are hypoallergenic and biocompatible.

Tooth Crystal Pricing

Pricing varies based on the type of gem selected. Standard crystals are very affordable, while genuine diamonds carry a premium.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tooth jewelry safe for my teeth?

Yes, tooth jewelry is a non-invasive, pain-free cosmetic procedure that does not harm your natural teeth. No drilling is involved, and the crystal can be removed at any time without damage to the tooth.

What are diamond tooth implants?

Diamond tooth implants are a premium dental enhancement that involves placing a real diamond within a dental implant or crown. They offer a premium, long-lasting shine and are custom-designed for each patient.

What are the benefits of gold tooth crowns?

Gold tooth crowns are a symbol of luxury and durability. They are extremely durable and long-lasting, resistant to wear, chipping, and fractures. They require minimal tooth reduction compared to porcelain crowns and are hypoallergenic and biocompatible.

Dental Implants Treatment for a Naturally Beautiful Smile

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone beneath the gum line. It works as a tooth root and a crown is attached on top of it, giving you a naturally beautiful and functional replacement tooth.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

If you have a gap left by missing teeth, it can lead to a number of problems including:

Speech difficulties caused by the gap
Difficulty in chewing food properly
Wrinkled skin and sunken cheeks
Improper biting
Gum diseases due to food entrapment in the gap

Benefits of Dental Implants

Regain your biting strength
Speak properly and confidently
Look natural and feel confident
Bone saving — eliminate the risk of bone loss
Do not harm adjacent teeth (unlike bridges)

Post Implant Care

After getting dental implants, it is important to maintain proper oral hygiene. Brush twice daily using softer bristles, floss daily, and make frequent visits to your dentist for check-ups to ensure the longevity of your implants.

Frequently Asked Questions

The cost of a dental implant ranges from Rs.35,000 to Rs.45,000. The crown placed on top of the implant costs an additional Rs.10,000 to Rs.20,000, depending on the type of crown selected.

Dental implants have a success rate of over 92% over 10 years. With proper care, regular dental visits, and good oral hygiene, implants can last a lifetime.

Recovery after dental implant surgery can be as little as a week. Most patients are able to return to their normal routine within a few days, though complete osseointegration (bone fusion) takes a few months.

There is no upper age limit for getting dental implants. However, implants should be avoided before the age of 18–20 years, as the jawbone is still developing during that period.

World-Class Orthodontic Care

Orthodontic treatment goes far beyond straightening teeth. Properly aligned teeth improve your bite, make oral hygiene easier, reduce the risk of jaw joint disorders, and dramatically enhance your confidence. At Dr. Kathuria's Dentistry, our specialist orthodontists use the latest technology and treatment systems to deliver predictable, beautiful results.

Whether you prefer the discretion of invisible aligners, the efficiency of ceramic braces, or the complete invisibility of lingual braces, we have the perfect solution tailored to your lifestyle, age, and orthodontic needs.

Your Orthodontic Journey in 4 Steps

1

Free Consultation

2

3D scans, X-rays, and photographs are used to create a precise, customised treatment plan with predicted outcomes.

3

4

After treatment, retainers maintain your new smile. You enjoy perfectly aligned teeth for a lifetime.

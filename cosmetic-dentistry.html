Factors That Influence Our Smile Design Procedures

Facial Shape

Cosmetic procedures are designed and customised as per the shape of a person's face. A long face may require accentuating the width in order to make it appear more oval.

Image Aspirations

How do you wish to appear to others or yourself in the mirror? Do you wish to have a sophisticated smile or one which displays boldness and energy? We perform cosmetic dental procedures that suit your face, personality, appearance and requirements.

Age

Cosmetic procedures also depend on age. While whiter teeth will make you appear younger, a subdued colour or a flattering smile shall make you look more mature in age.

Time Constraint

The time involved in procedures of cosmetic dentistry varies from a single visit to a couple of months or even years. While some treatments can be completed in an hour, others may take more time.

Got Questions?

Common FAQs

What are the causes behind the discolouration of teeth?

There are various causes that can discolour teeth internally and externally. While drinking beverages like wine, tea or coffee and chewing tobacco can cause external staining, consumption of excessive fluoride during tooth formation, medical treatments can stain teeth internally.

Are there any side effects of bleaching?

Although bleaching is a safe procedure, you may experience a few short-lived side effects such as tingling sensations or sensitivity. However, it does not last for too long. In the worst-case scenario, mild sensitivity may be a maximum of 24 hours but can be countered by the use of a medicated mouthwash, toothpaste or anti-inflammatory tablet.

Does cosmetic contouring hurt?

No, cosmetic contouring is painless, does not require anaesthesia and has no recovery time after the treatment is completed.

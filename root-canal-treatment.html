About This Treatment

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment (RCT) is a procedure used to treat a damaged tooth by removing the inflamed or infected pulp, thereby relieving pain and saving the natural tooth. The pulp is the soft tissue inside the tooth that contains blood vessels, nerves, and connective tissue.

At Dr. Kathuria's Dentistry, root canal treatments are performed by our team of TRAINED ENDODONTISTS (RCT Specialists) using advanced microscopes and rotary instruments for precise, single-sitting procedures.

Causes of Root Canal Infection

  • Faulty crowns
  • Deep decay
  • A crack or chip in the tooth
  • Multiple dental procedures on the same tooth

Symptoms That You May Need a Root Canal

  • Pain in teeth
  • Swelling on face or neck
  • Tenderness in gums
  • Pus draining from the affected area
  • Sensitivity to hot and cold
  • Severe ache while biting
  • Darkening of the tooth

Benefits of Root Canal Treatment

  • No inter-appointment bacterial leakage
  • Less consuming procedure
  • Painless and cost-effective
  • Solves chewing issues
  • Restores normal biting force and sensation
  • Enhances natural appearance
  • Protects surrounding teeth from excessive wear
How It Works

The Root Canal Process

1

Diagnosis & Imaging

Digital X-ray or 3D CBCT scan to assess infection extent and map the root canal anatomy. Local anaesthesia ensures complete numbness before we begin.

2

Access & Cleaning

Using the dental microscope, a small access opening is made. Rotary NiTi files carefully remove infected nerve tissue and bacteria from every canal.

3

Disinfection & Shaping

Canals are shaped and irrigated with antimicrobial solutions to eliminate all bacteria. Ultrasonic activation ensures thorough disinfection of the canal system.

4

Filling & Crown

Canals are sealed with biocompatible material. A custom-made ceramic crown is placed to protect and restore the tooth to full function and appearance.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

No, root canal treatment is painless. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during the treatment. In fact, the root canal relieves the severe pain caused by the infected pulp.

How long does a single sitting root canal take?

A single sitting root canal treatment takes approximately 40 minutes. At Dr. Kathuria's Dentistry, our trained endodontists complete most root canal cases in a single visit using advanced rotary instruments and dental microscopes.

What is the success rate of root canal treatment?

The success rate of root canal treatment ranges from 87% to 99%. At Dr. Kathuria's Dentistry, our success rate is 99% thanks to our trained endodontists, advanced microscopes, and state-of-the-art technology.

How much does a root canal cost?

Root canal treatment at Dr. Kathuria's Dentistry costs Rs.10,000 to Rs.20,000 per tooth. The cost varies based on the tooth location and complexity. Crown charges are separate and depend on the type of crown chosen — Ceramic, Porcelain fused to metal, or Base metal alloys.

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

In most cases, yes. A root canal-treated tooth becomes more brittle over time because it no longer receives blood supply. A crown protects the tooth from fracture and restores its full strength. Crown types include Ceramic (most common), Porcelain fused to metal (stronger and more durable), and Base metal alloys (corrosion resistant).

Pricing & Payment FAQs

Everything you need to know about our charges, payment options, and insurance.

The prices shown are indicative starting rates. Your final cost depends on the complexity of your case, materials selected (e.g., standard vs. premium zirconia), number of teeth involved, and the treatment plan designed by our specialists. After an in-clinic or video consultation, we provide a detailed, itemized cost sheet with no hidden fees.

Yes. For treatments above ₹20,000, we offer no-cost EMI options through select partner banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Bajaj Finserv). You can split your bill into 3, 6, 9, or 12 monthly installments. We also accept phased payments aligned with multi-visit treatment schedules.

We are empaneled with several major health insurance providers and TPA networks. We accept cashless claims where applicable and can also provide the necessary invoices and documentation for reimbursement claims. Please share your insurance details during booking, and our team will verify coverage before your appointment.

We accept cash (INR), all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, RuPay), UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), and international wire transfers. For international patients, we can also process payments in USD, GBP, EUR, and AUD.
